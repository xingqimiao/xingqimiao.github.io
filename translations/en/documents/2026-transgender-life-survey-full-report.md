## The full report

This is the complete published edition of the *2026 Survey Report on the Living Conditions of Transgender People in Chinese Online Communities*: **95 pages**, 12 chapters and 32 charts, with notes on method and a sensitivity analysis.

The main analysis covers **n=900** records; the age variable has **n=899** valid responses. A stricter scope that keeps only records with verifiable consent (**n=888 / age n=887**) is used only as a sensitivity comparison, never as the main analysis.

## What the report contains

- **12 chapters**: executive summary; background and sample characteristics; identity awareness and expression; body and healthcare; family; campus; employment and economy; mental health and well-being; documents and public services; social support and resilience; key findings and directions for action; appendix.
- **32 charts**: 26 result charts plus 6 exploratory charts. Single-choice questions are drawn as horizontal bars or donut charts, multiple-choice questions as horizontal bars, all on a 0–100% scale. On the website they are live, drawn charts; the PDF is the same layout in vector form.
- **Method notes** (Chapter 2): sampling, recruitment channels, data-processing rules and statistical methods.
- **Limitations** (Chapter 11): what the results can and cannot support, and why a non-significant test does not mean "no relationship".

## Version

- Current version: **v1.2**, released August 2026.
- What changed in this version: every chart is now drawn live in the browser rather than shipped as a bitmap, duplicated passages and internal identifiers were removed from the body text, and the method notes were rewritten in plainer language.
- The PDF and the web edition carry the same content; page counts differ because the web edition scrolls continuously.

## Limits of use

- This survey makes no clinical diagnosis; reported diagnoses are self-reported and were not verified against medical records.
- Public materials present aggregate results only, and **cannot be used to trace any individual response**.
- The main analysis excludes older records whose consent status could not be verified — an ethical-first handling given the available evidence. The sensitivity analysis does not make up for, or verify, that consent.
- Every "association" is a statistical association, not a causal one. The report is not a tool for judging any individual's situation or risk.
