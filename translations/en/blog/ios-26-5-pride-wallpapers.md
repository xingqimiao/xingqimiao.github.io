title: 'iOS 26.5 Pride wallpapers'
---

Apple today released a new Apple Watch Pride Edition Sport Band, a matching watch face, and iPhone and iPad wallpapers to celebrate Pride month and the ongoing worldwide support for the LGBTQ+ community. Apple is proud to fund organisations that serve LGBTQ+ people.

The new Pride Luminance watch face is designed to work with the band: its shifting, refracted colours express the vitality, spirit and individuality of the LGBTQ+ community. The face comes in two geometric designs — a radial pattern whose rays of colour line up with the hour markers, and a vertical pattern echoing the multicoloured stripes of the woven band. The collection offers more colour choices, so wearers can customise the face and make it their own.

With this collection, Apple Watch wearers can customise both the watch face and the matching iPhone and iPad wallpapers, with extra colour options that let people show their own personality and their community's.

The matching iPhone and iPad wallpapers use a lively, dynamic design, with the same customisable colours.
