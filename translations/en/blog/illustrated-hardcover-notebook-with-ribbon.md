We were handed a delicate palette from the start 🌈

Like the “hardcover with beautiful illustrations and a ribbon” in that quiz, **we are drawn to soft colours without being taught to be. In a crowd we instinctively soften our footsteps; our sympathy seems to spill over the edges, and a falling leaf or a single line of dialogue can bring tears**🥺

Many of us, from the moment we were born, had a different book shoved into our hands: a thick black business notebook…

That heavy, cold black leather was the shell we were forced to live in. We were told to be rough, and made to press every longing for softness down under that hard cover.

**Inside a black notebook that was never ours, late at night when nobody could see, we wrote the real self over and over in tears — and then closed it in a hurry before dawn, pretending nothing had happened…**

![illustrated-hardcover-notebook-with-ribbon1](/pic/blog/illustrated-hardcover-notebook-with-ribbon-02.png)

So when we finally walk bravely into a stationery shop and pick out the diary with the soft, fresh cover… remember: this was never a performance staged for the world. It is not a label stuck on to earn someone’s approval. It is a soul that had been locked in a dark, cold room for too long, finally pushing the window open — greedily, carefully — to embrace the **sunlight and the breeze** that were hers all along 😿

Whether that cover is plain or riotously colourful, once you open it, what lies inside is **never** what you “ought” to be. It is a soul that, after being broken, still insists on being **brilliant and brave** 🏳️‍🌈
