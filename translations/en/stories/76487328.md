title: For the Sun of Tomorrow
---

I do not wish to sink into a handful of yellow earth, nor to circle through the cycles of grass, wood, birds and beasts, tasting again this world's beauty and clamor. I ask only for a silent vanishing — a farewell undisturbed, no one remembering, no one weeping. If my wish is granted, carry me up on the wind, and let me gaze one last time at the land where I made my home; accompany me in diving to the deep sea, to encounter those ancient traces that have slept for ten million years. I am greedy for the most extreme romance, yet also deep in an inborn pessimism. If this world cannot hold such a thought, then grant me a silent dissolution — entangling no one, adding not a trace of worry; let time gently erase me, let memory utterly forget me, until my form and my mood both return to boundless silence. Companionship with loneliness, long sleep, sweet dreams… — Xiao He. Reality is a complete disaster; the virtual is flawless. Then why live in this world? For hope, I suppose — for tomorrow's sun on my face. (My mental state is now relatively optimistic.)
