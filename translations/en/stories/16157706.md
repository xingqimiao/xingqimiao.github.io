---
title: The Kingdom on the Wasteland
---

We have been judged without cause and without fairness in the social arena; society exiled us to the wilderness, where we are lashed by long winds and battered by storms. So we need to be there for each other, and we need to find a way to put down roots in this soil-less wasteland. Please keep your compassion. Please be strong and brave. Live with pride, cross over despair, pain and death, and build our "kingdom" on the ruins. — Yuehen
