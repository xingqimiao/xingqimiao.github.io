---
title: The Wine-Soaked Needle, the Spirit Healer, and an Absurd Obsession
---

Out of despair, I once came out with a nothing-to-lose attitude, then went back deep in the closet. I'm hoping to gain semi-independence (university) as soon as possible, to avoid the risk of conversion treatment. During the coming-out period, my parents sought help from relatives — a way out — and then took me almost a hundred kilometers to see a "magic immortal" (folk healer). The "immortal" had my mother soak a needle in wine and place it in my room, with a bowl of water beside it; the next morning I was to drink that bowl of water, and gather the needle to show him later. He wanted us to do this for three days, then stop two days, then repeat for three days.

Under the inducement of a "hospital broker" (medical tour guide), my parents took me to a private hospital for psychiatric evaluation, aiming to "cure" me (I'm actually grateful to that broker — otherwise the evaluation would never have happened; before this I believed I was unworthy of and couldn't possibly have an evaluation). Afterwards, my parents — holding the report showing severe OCD, moderate depression, and moderate anxiety — insisted I had nothing wrong, and went home disappointed that I hadn't been "cured". At the time they refused injection treatment and said: "A good beating will fix you."

Both of them dropped out of middle school. We're from a rural area in Northeast China. My father started construction work at 17 and did it all his life; my mother joined him working when I was in sixth grade. Since then I've mostly been looked after by my maternal grandmother (my paternal grandparents have passed). I once thought about running away, but didn't — because of all the restrictions society places on minors (afraid I couldn't survive). Currently I plan to endure at least until university, when I reach a semi-independent state; if my parents still object, I'll leave the family then.
