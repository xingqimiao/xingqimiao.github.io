title: The Resonant Tuning Fork and the Sprouting Branch

Actually, it's only the beautification of facts that counts as true instigation and propaganda. Seeing someone share OD and self-harm doesn't bother me — it's a way of venting that hurts no one else. Not expressing emotions not only fails to reduce the negative impact of "emotional contagion", it can actually intensify the feelings and cause us to miss the window for outside help. And there's no need to compare who's better — doing what you love is what matters most, what has meaning.

> Reminder: drug abuse and self-harm can cause serious physical and psychological damage; please do not attempt or imitate. If you or someone around you is facing such distress, contact professional institutions for help in time.
