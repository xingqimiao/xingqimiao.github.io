title: The Diagnosis Certificate Nobody Understood
---

I started HRT on 26.4.12. On 26.7.5 my parents went through my schoolbag and found the HRT drugs. They cursed at me and hit me, and wanted to subject me to conversion therapy. Under my guidance, they took me to Dr. Di Xiaolan, who gave me the transsexualism diagnosis. Despite Dr. Di's persuasion, they refused to take me to Peking University Third Hospital to prescribe HRT, and warned me they'd break my legs if I bought the medication again.
