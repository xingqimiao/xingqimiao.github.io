---
title: Finding a Corner to Live Quietly at the Heart of the City's Splendor
---

Words to the world: even in a first-tier city, a discordant family will affect a child for their whole life. A child longs so much for a harmonious family — it's too hard, and it has affected too much: studies, job hunting, the whole person has gone slack. I only wish for a good life, to happily be myself.
