---
title: Gentle Resonance Across the Dinner Table
---

Last time I went out to eat with my family — because I have long hair, a fairly androgynous look, and the new toiletries I'd been using — maybe blood runs thicker than water, and my mom asked me: "I feel your life habits moving more and more toward the feminine. Do you want to be a girl?" My feelings at that moment were tangled; I didn't know what my family thought, but how we treat each other matters so much to me, so I answered: "How could that be! I just think this look suits me." Then she replied: "Haha! I see — looks like I was overthinking. But, you're my flesh and blood; even if you wanted to transition, I would support you." I was deeply moved on the spot, but I felt conflicted: "Should I tell them? Or was it just a joke?" I'm still wondering even now — if I truly confessed, how would they see me?
