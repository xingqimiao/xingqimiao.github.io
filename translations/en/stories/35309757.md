---
title: Love That Sprouted
---

Honestly, the way I got together with my partner was really wonderful. About a year ago, she confessed her feelings to me. Back then my gender dysphoria was severe and I was very insecure, always feeling I didn't deserve her — I even told her outright that I wanted to become a girl and asked her to give up on me. (I only learned later that she's a lesbian.) But she said to me: "You ARE a cute girl. If you really can't accept being this way right now, I'll keep waiting — until you become who you dream of being." Even though I don't pass at all and am not cute, and I don't know whether her answer was truly sincere — at least I chose to believe it. It was precisely because of her support that I could boldly start HRT. If there's anything to tell my sisters: if someone as ugly, untalented, and dim-witted as me can meet a partner who supports them, then you are all better and harder-working than me, so you will definitely find the love you dream of!
