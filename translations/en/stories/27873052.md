---
title: The Forced Long Leave
---

> Reader note: this piece contains suicide content.
> Don't only ask why a person in despair chose the wrong escape; see what pushed them into a place with no right exit. If you're in a dark place right now, please don't force yourself to read this. Protecting your own heart is always what matters most.

A classmate at my school, an mtx in the same grade, fell into a suicidal crisis and asked me for help. I agonized over it for a long time, tried my best to talk them out of it, but still failed to persuade them. Then, one after another, both of us acted on self-harm (mine from my own reasons compounded with guilt). We were both rescued, and both forced to take a long leave of absence.
