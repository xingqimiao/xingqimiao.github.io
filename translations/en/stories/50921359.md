I am [nickname hidden]. At age 11, I was violated. After family bankruptcy, I was beaten until I sat on the windowsill, wanting to jump. After that, the [nickname hidden] in my body handed the reins to me — a boy. She was the original; I came later. Neither of us disappeared; we just share one body. In grades 7 and 8, my Chinese teacher said my writing was good and encouraged me to write argumentative essays. In grade 9 the teacher changed; then there was only dictation and formulaic material. I like math because it never changes face, unlike life. My mom knows my identity and has talked about taking me to get puberty blockers — but she's dragged it out for half a year. I couldn't take it; since early February this year I've been using hormones on my own. My periods stopped, and my body has finally started moving in the "right" direction. But we fought, we raged, we called the police, we signed agreements. She says she loves me, but her love knows how to stab. I have friends. They joined me on a half-hour hunt for an accessible toilet, and they didn't run away when I lost my mind. They make me feel this broken Earth Online isn't worth deleting my account over yet. I have a little over a month until the high-school entrance exam. I don't know if I'll get into a key school, whether I can wear boys' uniforms in high school, or whether my mom will really take me to the hospital. But I know one thing: I'm not a monster. I'm a boy still carving himself — unfinished, but refusing to stop. Thank you for reading this far.
