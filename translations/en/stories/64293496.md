---
title: Walking Out of the Maze Like a Dam Bursting
---

About the question asking at what age I confirmed my gender identity: since childhood I had the idea of wanting to be a girl, but I always talked myself out of it. As a kid I read *Dream of the Red Chamber*, and when I saw that even Jia Baoyu said, "When I see girls, I feel refreshed; when I see men, I find them stinking and oppressive," I figured: well then, every boy must hate being a boy too! So I just muddled along, letting myself be. I dawdled until I was 18; one day, out of nowhere, I told my then-girlfriend I wanted to get sex-change surgery. She said people who do transition surgery die in their thirties. So I talked myself out of it again — I was even cowardly enough not to look it up online. Later I learned what LGBT is, and even argued online with people who were homophobic and transphobic, yet I was still silly enough to never look inside my own heart. It wasn't until I later went abroad that I accidentally wandered into RLE.wiki one day — and the dysphoria hit me like a dam bursting, washing me senseless. By then I was already 23.
