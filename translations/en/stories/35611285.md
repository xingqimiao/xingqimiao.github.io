---
title: Driving Alone Through the Dark Night
---

I was once held in a detention centre for 37 days after my political comments were defined as "picking quarrels and provoking trouble", and was finally released on bail pending trial. I grew up in a family with no relatives and no support; I held on entirely on my own willpower. So I consider myself a very steadfast, strong person — but in reality I have almost no friends; only my ex-girlfriend keeps accompanying me and understanding me, even though we're a thousand miles apart now. In my heart I still hope to make a few friends — even just to play games together sometimes. I'm writing as the thoughts come, so I'll stop here for now.
