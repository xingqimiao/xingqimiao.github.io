---
title: The Blooming Season That Will Come
---

We all have a blooming season — earlier or later; as long as we nurture it, it will eventually open. It has been ten years since I came out to my parents. I once suffered depression, dropped out of school, and stood at the edge of ending myself several times. Until two years ago, when I truly faced my gender dysphoria and embraced myself — I finally made it to renewal and blossoming. With serenity, accept what cannot be changed; with courage, change what can be changed; with wisdom, tell the two apart.
