title: The Light Is Breaking — Change Is Here
---

Before senior year of high school I had no transgender feelings or signs whatsoever — a typical cisgender straight boy. Around senior year, I was still a "leftist" online, and I read Engels' *The Origin of the Family, Private Property and the State*. The book hit me hard, but unlike other online leftists, after reading it I began to hate men, to hate my own assigned male identity. A gender anxiety I didn't yet recognize began to build, and I wished to become a "revolutionary" woman. So I changed my gender to female on every platform, interacted with online friends as a passing girl, learned about feminism — and it was only through gradually coming to understand LGBTQIA+ that I realized I was, in fact, transgender.
