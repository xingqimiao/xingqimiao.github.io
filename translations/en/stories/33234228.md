---
title: The Lives I've Met
---

I don't have any particularly special experience, but after starting to communicate with my kind, I've met people who started HRT at just over ten years old; people over thirty, nearly forty, still unable to afford SRS; people whose parents supported them wholeheartedly and were truly happy; and people who, because the real-world family and friends never understood them, finally chose to go to death with a calm acceptance. Everyone has their own difficulties, their own life. What I want to say to all my kind: love yourself well, let yourself shine — you will definitely receive the love that belongs to you.
