---
title: Barely Clinging On — But Picking Up the Paintbrush
---

Because of severe mental illness that began in early middle school for all kinds of reasons, I barely hung on until university, then dropped out at the end of the first semester! Now I'm working hard to become an outstanding artist — that way I can win more visibility for groups like enby, who have been systematically ignored for so long. Coming out publicly as trans probably means someday I'll hit the glass ceiling, but overall this part is still my own stubborn effort and one of my biggest sources of motivation.
