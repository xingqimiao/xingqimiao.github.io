---
title: The Spectrum Beyond the Binary
---

I want people to understand that gender is not simply male or female. In this world, some people's gender differentiation naturally falls into neither category. We are a third gender — existing naturally, not subject to ideological shifts. As a group that cannot easily do ordinary manual labour like most people, and who must retreat from the public eye, we need to unite to win empowerment opportunities, contributing more technical and artistic value. The "effeminate man" carries half the sky: reject the sissy stereotype forced on us by the far right.
