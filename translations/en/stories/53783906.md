---
title: The Gender Column Left Blank
---

In 2022, before dropping out of a top high school, I wrote a coming-out letter to the grade director. Four girls saw it. They drew a male penis and asked me if I knew what it was. In 2025, I resumed study at a vocational college. My name was missing from the physical-exam registration list; after the class monitor added my information, she insisted I write down the gender myself.
