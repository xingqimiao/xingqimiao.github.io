title: Swamped in Troubles, Forced Out of School
---

I was abandoned by my parents for reasons unrelated to my transgender identity, and these debuffs piled on top of each other... Because I feared the relative who actually raised me — with his outdated views — might refuse to keep raising me after I was "blown out of the closet", I waited until 22 to even start HRT. And all that is nothing compared to the worst part: I can't go back to the big city where I was born to use its educational resources, because schools there don't allow boarding — and being abandoned by my parents leaves me nowhere to live. Also, if I sat the high-school entrance exam in my birthplace, I'd have to sue my parents for child support — but the relative raising me wants to shield one of my biological parents. So I was forced out of school. Sigh...
