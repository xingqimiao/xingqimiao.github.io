---
title: Skirts, Dolls, and the Late-Arriving Wholeness
---

Since childhood I've known I was different. Everyone said I looked like a girl, but no one thought I WAS a girl, and I also felt I should be with girls. I kept wondering what the difference between me and girls actually was. After learning, I've hated my genitals. I love skirts and dolls — but I had to force myself to like the things I believed boys should like. That feeling was truly painful. But now I've let go of much of it, and my body is improving too.
