title: The Lunch Hour That Made Me Cry with Anger

An admin on some streamer's chat expressed a view about LGBT: saying the topic isn't suitable for that chat room, since there are plenty of minors whose values aren't yet formed. Even educational content is a way of promoting the circle — once they're curious, they'll go look it up, and then it's beyond control. That day at lunch I was so furious I cried.
