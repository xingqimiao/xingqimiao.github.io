---
title: Breaking the Cocoon at Sixteen
---

I blew out of the closet at 16 and have been living by myself since — I've been through a lot. Now at 20 I'm self-employed, earning 4–6k a month (depending on how hard I work), my appearance is very well socially recognized, and apart from a certain lack of emotional connection, I don't differ much from a woman assigned female at birth.
