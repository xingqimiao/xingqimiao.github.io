---
title: Tenacity That Roots Deep Into the Ground
---

No matter what caused your gender identity to differ or feel unclear, you must keep your own will tenacious. I recommend choosing to be open only after you've achieved financial independence and sufficient ability to withstand pressure — otherwise it's easy to develop psychological problems from stress and discrimination. If your family doesn't support or won't allow it, then endure and lie low. Don't fantasize that they'll suddenly accept it one day; don't depend on them long-term either. Through study or learning a trade, get as far away from the source of trouble as you can — the farther the better. Finally: don't OD, don't go into sex work, don't overuse hormone medications, and please don't harm yourself. Don't trust certain people on Twitter or in other communities who might push you into hurting yourself, and don't wallow in self-despair. No difficulty is unsurvivable — grit your teeth and you will get through. Trust me: as a former exam student from the "Four Mountain-Hard Provinces", I made it through too, and I'm doing fairly well now.
