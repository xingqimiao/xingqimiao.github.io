title: Rain Stops — Sheathe the Umbrella as a Sword
---

I'm ftm, attracted to women. My earliest memory at age three is a gentle, beautiful older sister I met in the hospital, where I was sick. As a baby I showed unusual attachment to breasts. Since childhood, my personality and interests were masculine; my friends were mostly boys. In middle school I realized I was transgender. Severe depression ran through my entire adolescence; my family didn't accept it. Being clever and hard-working, I got into a 985 university and gradually walked out of the depression. Once I chatted with a French person, who described what it's like to be transgender in France — not being oppressed and discriminated against as in China. Transgender has brought me much trauma, and much gain. Just... living.
