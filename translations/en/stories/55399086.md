---
title: Living Under a Different Identity
---

Once, this was the hardest period of my life — four major blows in a row, to the point I basically lost my hold on living. I hurt myself in many ways; sometimes nothing happened, only the pain proving this was real. There was even a time I saw the tunnel with the light at the end, yet I still didn't die. Although things have stabilized a lot now… sometimes I still fall into anxiety. I hope I can slowly get better — since I've already made my decision: to live under a different identity.
