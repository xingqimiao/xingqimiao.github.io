---
title: The Ordinary Road Is the Answer
---

Once I crossed mountains and seas / and walked through crowds of people / all I once had / scattered like smoke in a blink / once lost, disappointed, lost every direction / until I saw that the ordinary is the only answer / once I destroyed all I had / wanting nothing but to leave forever / once I fell into boundless darkness / struggling, unable to pull free / once I was like you, like him, like that wild grass and wild flower / despairing, and yearning / crying and laughing, ordinary / once I crossed mountains and seas / and walked through crowds of people / once I asked this whole world / never getting an answer / I'm no more than you, than him, than that wild grass and wild flower / in the depths, this is me / the only road I have to walk.
