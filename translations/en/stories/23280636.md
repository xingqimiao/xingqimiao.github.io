---
title: There Will Always Be Something Worth Waiting For
---

Meow meow... My wish is that everyone walks this path safely and soundly, and that we can all stay happy, forever holding each other. I know I'll live well, because there will always be something worth looking forward to in the future... Like seeing in 2029 that asteroid skimming close to Earth; in 2030 that rocket launching from Wenchang toward the Moon; three total solar eclipses in Australia in the 2030s... Like seeing the day I live as a lovely girl myself. So no matter how hard it is right now, I'll do my best to live. I hope everyone can find their own visible thing to look forward to — that way you'll find the drive to live. May I offer the cats a *ci* poem: the wild geese carry cold frost through the curtain; the moon-holding clouds drift light, new ice still thin; the watery mirror reflects combing and smoothing; think of perfumed powder, bright cosmetics — hard to learn; jade skin so frail, layered over with dragon-silk; leaning in the east wind, a faint-smiling glance, myriads of flowers drown in shame. ♡ —Rain Antares
