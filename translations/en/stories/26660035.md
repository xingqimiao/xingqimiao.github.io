---
title: Trust Yourself — You'll Reach the End You Aimed For
---

I think the only reason I can keep on living is that I refuse to bow: why should I live badly? This is not my destiny. Carrying this in my heart, I take on every kind of challenge. I won't believe any label anyone puts on me — whether they call me abnormal, or incapable — those are just their eyes' judgment. I don't accept those shackles. Living in someone else's eyes is the same as handing them your eyes and your brain; it makes you lose direction. In this whole world, only I will always stand on my side — trust myself unconditionally, train myself without pause, take care of myself, love myself. The road ahead is long. Trust that you will reach the end you aimed for.
