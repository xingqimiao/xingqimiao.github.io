title: A Journey of Watching Each Other's Backs
---

Fight for all the beauty in the world! A while ago, I got my first ever hormone panel test. A sister took me there — we both got tested — and she gave me a little bead bracelet. I was so happy; it felt warm. Later I put the bracelet in my pencil case, took it with me to school — it's become my lucky charm, w. Meeting peers face-to-face is the most relaxed and pleasant kind of offline socializing there is, but it also often makes my chest ache — accompanied by a certain warm feeling...  I've genuinely been helped a great deal by the offline mutual-aid actions of trans women. I hope everyone can actively build mutual-aid communities. — akizakura
